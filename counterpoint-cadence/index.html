Counterpoint Cadence

A Free Paired-Feed Campaign Framework

Cross-posting repeats a message. Counterpoint Cadence composes one.

Download the Free Framework PDF

Direct PDF link: ../downloads/Counterpoint_Cadence_Free_Framework.pdf

Counterpoint Cadence is a free framework for publishing one connected campaign across two accounts without making either account a duplicate of the other.

One surface carries the source signal:

lived context, original thought, memory, behind-the-scenes material, or direct reflection.

The other carries the public frame:

analysis, principle, practical application, or a broader argument.

Cadence

Opposite Directions, Shared Rhythm

Both publish on the same cadence.

But they move through the sequence in opposite directions.

Surface A:

1 → 2 → 3 → 4

Surface B:

4 → 3 → 2 → 1

This creates a distributed narrative. Each account stands on its own, but people following both experience connection, contrast, pressure, and eventual convergence.
