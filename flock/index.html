These documents are the starting record for this collection. Statements recorded in minutes identify what speakers said; they do not turn every statement into an independently established fact or a court ruling.

Editorial approach

A position readers can examine

I oppose broad surveillance of ordinary travel. That position carries a responsibility to distinguish the record from my interpretation, identify unresolved questions, and correct claims when the evidence requires it.

This collection begins with one source-linked Wells County article. Further commentary can build on that record without making readers reconstruct the discussion from scattered social-media threads.
