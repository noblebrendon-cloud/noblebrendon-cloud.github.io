The Letter of Release

Ecclesiastes 3:6 — A time to search and a time to give up.

Letter

Read The Letter

Today you are invited to release what no longer serves your purpose.

Release is not loss — it is the clearing of space for what is meant to arrive.

What you hold too tightly becomes a weight. What you release with intention becomes a seed.

Let go of the outcome. Trust the process. Your hands were not made to grip — they were made to create.

Reflect

Questions For The Reader

What is one thing you have been holding onto that no longer serves you?

If you released your biggest fear today, what would you do tomorrow?

What does 'letting go' look like in your daily life?

Who in your life models healthy release — and what have you learned from them?

Write one sentence of permission to yourself. Begin with: 'I release...'
