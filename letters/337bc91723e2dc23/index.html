The Letter of Gratitude

1 Thessalonians 5:18 — Give thanks in all circumstances.

Letter

Read The Letter

Give thanks before increase.

Gratitude is the foundation of abundance.

When you name what you have — truly name it — scarcity loses its grip on your mind.

Today, before you ask for more, honor what is already here.

The ground beneath you held. The breath in your lungs continued. That alone is worth a letter.

Reflect

Questions For The Reader

Name three things you have right now that you once prayed for.

Who is someone you have never properly thanked?

What ordinary moment today deserves your full attention?

How does gratitude change the way you see what you lack?

Write one sentence of thanks for something you usually overlook.
