A Letters of Light Devotional

Remember the Birds

Trusting God's provision while faithfully doing what the day requires.

By Brendon R. Coleman Published November 18, 2026
Back to Letters of Light

Remember the birds.

They rise each morning with no storehouses and no plans beyond the day, yet the Lord provides for them without fail. And if He so cares for them, how much more does He care for us?

When moments of distress press in, let us walk in godly wisdom — doing what the day requires, and no more. Every day has its own portion, and worrying for tomorrow only blinds us to what God is already giving us here and now.

By being wise stewards of the day at hand, we slip past the anxieties this world tries to place on our shoulders. We rest in faith — not passive, but trusting — knowing that the Father sees our needs long before we speak them.

And when we offer Him our small acts of prudence, humility, and obedience, we meet Him at the same threshold where the birds are kept.

A place of dependence.

A place of simplicity.

A place where provision is not earned, but given.

Scriptural foundation: Matthew 6:25–34 KJV, particularly verses 26 and 34.
