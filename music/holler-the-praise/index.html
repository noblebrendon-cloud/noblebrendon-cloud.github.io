A ten-song devotional journey

Holler the Praise

Worship shaped by the hills, the road, firelight, storms, hunger, prayer, and praise.

January 25, 2026 10 tracks NobleWorksLLC
The album

Before it was a release, it was a place I kept returning to.

Holler the Praise grew out of a simple posture: make worship from the world as it was actually being lived.

The project first took shape under the working language “Holler Gospel.” The word carried two meanings at once: the Appalachian hollow, and the act of crying something out. The sound followed that same tension—Christian worship at the center, carried through folk, roots, and blues rather than polished contemporary worship conventions.

The songs moved through roads, hills, water, fire, wind, storms, hunger, ash, prayer, and finally hallelujah. This page follows them in their published order, not to flatten them into one explanation, but to preserve the path they made together.
